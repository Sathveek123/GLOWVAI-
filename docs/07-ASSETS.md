# Assets

## Product photography (real product only)
- Phone main lens, 4:5 vertical, morning window light, white card as bounce, no flash.
- White sweep backdrop. Wipe fingerprints, label straight, dropper filled.
- Shots: front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, flatlay of 3 products, texture swatch,
  hand holding product (no face).
- Remove the background for the front shot, keep a soft shadow layer, export WebP q80 under 150KB.

File names (use exactly these):
hero-product-front.webp, hero-product-34-left.webp, hero-swatch.webp, hero-flatlay.webp, hero-hand.webp
config/assets.ts maps them. The hero loads hero-product-front.webp.

## AI images: allowed and not allowed
Allowed: backgrounds, textures, abstract shapes, botanical props.
Not allowed: the product itself (it must match what ships), faces presented as founders or
customers, packaging text or logos.
Starter prompts:
1. Soft blue and white abstract paper-cut organic shapes, subtle grain, studio light, seamless background, no text
2. Macro close-up of a translucent skincare gel smear on white glass, side lighting, tiny bubbles, no text
3. Top-down flatlay of fresh green Centella leaves and water droplets on pale blue silk, natural daylight, 4:5

## Hygiene
Keep a sheet of image, source, and license. No hotlinking. The logo is an SVG.
