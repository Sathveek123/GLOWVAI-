# GLOW VAI — AI Image Generation Starter Prompts

This guide lists prompts for generating supplementary textures, ingredient swatches, and studio props.

> [!NOTE]
> AI-generated imagery is permitted ONLY for background textures, macro swatches, and generic props. Never use AI-generated human faces presented as actual founders or customers. Packaging logos and label typography must be overlaid in design software after generation for brand accuracy.

---

## 🎨 Recommended Starter Prompts

### 1. Hero Bottle Cutout
```text
Studio product photo of a minimal frosted glass serum bottle with a white dropper, soft window light from the left, clean white background, gentle contact shadow, 4:5, no text, no logo, high detail
```

### 2. Gel Swatch Macro
```text
Macro close-up of a translucent skincare gel swatch smeared on a white glass surface, side lighting, visible texture, tiny bubbles, neutral background, no text
```

### 3. Abstract Background Blobs
```text
Soft blue and white abstract paper-cut organic shapes, subtle grain, studio light, seamless background, no text
```

### 4. Botanical Ingredient Flatlay
```text
Top-down studio flatlay of fresh green Centella leaves and water droplets on pale blue silk, natural daylight, soft shadows, 4:5
```
