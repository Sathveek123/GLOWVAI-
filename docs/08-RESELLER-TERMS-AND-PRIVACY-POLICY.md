# GLOW VAI Reseller & Earn Partner Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

## PART I: GLOW VAI RESELLER PROGRAM TERMS OF SERVICE

### 1. Program Overview & Eligibility
1.1. **The Program**: GLOW VAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("GLOW VAI", "Company", "We", "Us") operates the GLOW VAI Reseller & Earn Partner Program (the "Program") via `earn.glowvai.in`. The Program allows approved independent individuals and business entities ("Resellers", "Partners", "You") to promote GLOW VAI skincare diagnostic services and curated products (including Minimalist and The Derma Co formulations) via unique referral links, WhatsApp order intents, and social commerce channels.

1.2. **Eligibility Requirements**: To enroll as a Reseller, You must:
- Be an Indian resident aged 18 years or older with full legal capacity to enter into binding contracts.
- Possess a valid Indian Permanent Account Number (PAN) and active bank account in Your name for payout clearance.
- Agree to maintain full compliance with all applicable Indian commercial, tax, e-commerce, and data protection laws.

### 2. Account Registration & KYC Verification
2.1. **Registration**: You must submit accurate, complete registration details via `https://earn.glowvai.in/portal.html`.
2.2. **KYC & Verification**: GLOW VAI reserves the right to request Know Your Customer (KYC) documentation, including PAN, Aadhaar (masked), and bank account verification prior to releasing commission payouts exceeding ₹1,000.
2.3. **Account Security**: You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of Your reseller portal login credentials and for all activities conducted through Your account.

### 3. Referral Mechanism & WhatsApp Order Intents
3.1. **Order Tracking**: Orders are attributed to Resellers via:
- Unique tracking link parameters (`?ref=YOUR_RESELLER_ID`).
- WhatsApp Order Reference codes (`GV-YYMMDD-XXXX`) generated during client cart checkout or scan routine selection.
3.2. **WhatsApp Order Intents**: When a customer initiates a WhatsApp order intent (`createWhatsAppOrderUrl`), GLOW VAI logs non-blocking telemetry (`OrderIntents` tab) to attribute customer order references (`GV-YYMMDD-XXXX`) to the originating partner channel.
3.3. **Unconfirmed Intents**: An order intent alone does not guarantee commission. Commissions accrue strictly upon verified order fulfillment, customer payment clearance, and expiration of the standard 7-day return window.

### 4. Commission Structure, Payouts & Taxation
4.1. **Commission Rate**: Commissions are calculated as a fixed percentage of net sales value (excluding government taxes, delivery fees, discounts, and refunded items) as specified in Your partner dashboard.
4.2. **Payout Schedule**: Commission payouts are processed on a bi-weekly cycle (15th and last calendar day of each month) for settled balances exceeding the minimum payout threshold of ₹500.
4.3. **Taxation & TDS**: 
- GLOW VAI shall deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194H / 194-O of the Income Tax Act, 1961, at applicable statutory rates prior to payout.
- Form 16A TDS certificates shall be issued quarterly for registered partners.
- Resellers are independently responsible for reporting income and fulfilling applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST) obligations if applicable.

### 5. Prohibited Marketing Practices & Code of Conduct
Resellers explicitly agree NOT to engage in any of the following prohibited activities:
5.1. **False Medical Claims**: Making therapeutic, medical, disease-cure, or diagnostic guarantees regarding GLOW VAI products or skin analysis scores. All skin analysis reports must be represented strictly as cosmetic wellness advice.
5.2. **Spam & Misleading Advertising**: Sending unsolicited bulk messages, spamming WhatsApp groups, or using deceptive clickbait titles.
5.3. **Brand Infringement & Domain Squatting**: Purchasing domain names, social media handles, or paid search keywords (e.g., Google Ads) containing "GLOW VAI", "GlowVai", or confusingly similar trademarks without prior written authorization.
5.4. **Self-Referrals**: Generating artificial commissions through self-orders or fraudulent loop accounts.

### 6. Intellectual Property & Brand Assets
6.1. **Limited License**: GLOW VAI grants Resellers a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use official logos, product copy decks, and promotional assets available in the Earn Portal strictly for authorized marketing purposes.
6.2. **Ownership**: All right, title, and interest in GLOW VAI branding, trade dress, website assets, skin analysis algorithms, and database schemas remain the exclusive intellectual property of GLOW VAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

### 7. Limitation of Liability & Indemnification
7.1. **Liability Cap**: In no event shall GLOW VAI's cumulative liability to any Reseller exceed the total commissions paid or payable to such Reseller in the three (3) months preceding the claim.
7.2. **Indemnification**: Resellers agree to defend, indemnify, and hold harmless GLOW VAI, its directors, employees, and affiliates from any third-party claims, penalties, or damages arising out of Reseller's breach of these Terms, misrepresentations, or violation of applicable laws.

## PART II: RESELLER PRIVACY POLICY (DPDP ACT 2023 COMPLIANT)

### 1. Data Controller & Contact Information
GLOW VAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd. acts as the Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").
- **Privacy Office**: `privacy@glowvai.in`
- **Data Protection Officer (DPO)**: Privacy Compliance Officer, GLOW VAI Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

### 2. Categories of Reseller Data Collected
We collect the following personal data necessary to manage the Reseller relationship:
- **Identity & Contact Data**: Full legal name, business name, WhatsApp mobile number, email address, postal address.
- **Financial & Tax Data**: Bank account details (account number, IFSC code), PAN card number, GSTIN (if applicable).
- **Performance Telemetry**: Unique referral URL hits, WhatsApp order intent reference logs (`GV-YYMMDD-XXXX`), commission payout history, conversion rates.

### 3. Purpose of Processing & Legal Basis
Reseller personal data is processed strictly for legitimate commercial purposes:
- Verification of partner identity and fraud prevention (Legal Obligation & Contractual Necessity).
- Calculation, tracking, and disbursement of commission payouts (Contractual Necessity).
- Filing tax returns and deducting TDS with statutory tax authorities (Legal Obligation under Income Tax Act).
- Transmitting operational updates, payout receipts, and program announcements via WhatsApp or email (Consent & Legitimate Use).

### 4. Zero Customer Face Image Access
- **Client Privacy Protection**: Resellers DO NOT receive, view, or have access to customer face scan photographs or raw biometric images captured during the Gen Z Skin Analysis process (`/face-analysis`).
- **Local Browser Analysis**: All selfie pixel calculations occur strictly client-side within the customer's browser. No raw customer face images are accessible to Resellers or stored on reseller dashboards.

### 5. Third-Party Data Sharing & Sub-processors
GLOW VAI does not sell or lease Reseller personal data. Data is shared exclusively with:
- **Banking & Payout Partners**: RBI-regulated banks and payment gateways (e.g., Razorpay, Cashfree) for direct bank transfers.
- **Tax Authorities**: Income Tax Department of India for TDS filing.
- **Cloud Infrastructure**: Secure, encrypted cloud servers hosted within Indian data centers.

### 6. Data Security, Retention & Partner Rights
6.1. **Security**: We implement AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit for all reseller financial records.
6.2. **Retention**: Reseller registration and tax records are retained for a minimum of 7 years following account closure to satisfy statutory tax audit requirements.
6.3. **Reseller Rights**: Resellers may exercise rights under the DPDP Act 2023, including right to access summary data, right to correction/updating, and right to grievance redressal by emailing `privacy@glowvai.in`.
